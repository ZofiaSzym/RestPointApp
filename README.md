# RestPointApp

Rest Point is a Unity project created to make a calming space for people to come to every day. At first, it is a mood tracker with a simple monthly calendar that shows you in colors how you felt in the past days of the month. You can pick from eight different moods. But it is not only that! The main purpose of Rest Point was to group all calming exercises that I or my loved ones personally use in the form of one app. There are breathing exercises that have calmed me from the border of a panic attack so many times I can't count. There is a painting tool that my mother, a teacher, uses with her students. There is a writing and destroying tool that two of my best friends use when they have their heads full. There are exact videos that carried me through studying for my final high school exams. There are calming sounds that psychology textbooks recommend. And there is also a grounding exercise that is highly recommended to come back from a panic state. I tried to create it as easy to use and with calming, not overwhelming colors as possible, so it actually can help people.
